### Dressing up the banana

> By requiring logging and declarations we are creating temptations for bidders to dress up their bananas. This would happen even if outright deception was not used, because unscrupulous bidders could interpret the requirements to follow the letter but not the spirit. It would even happen if the scrupulous bidders followed the instructions, since these steps would be interpreted as signs that there are no defects in the requirements. Admitting that uncertainty exists, with consequences on pricing, avoids believing that the banana peel can tell us more information than it actually can.

(positions §16, recorded 2026-10-10)
