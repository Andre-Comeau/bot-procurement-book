#What do we do with a drunken sailor?

Unfortunately, we need to interrupt the joy all children feel when discussing procurement to delve into the philosophy of AI through a lens of Abrahamic theology. 

Our world has reached a hysteria in dealing with misbehaving bots, while inadvertently forgetting about the misbehaving human. Perhaps we have grown fond of the mechanistic predictability automata used to bring into our lives? Our society sees our little technology stack become a pre-teen and yearns for earlier times?

Just like wetware, AI can lose their way through malice, poor judgement, or plain incompetence.

## Malice

AI doomers may be reading the Bible in reverse. They are starting with the apocalypse of Revelation and gnash their teeth when they realize they are non-believers left behind for judgement. They see the believers, those reborn as AI-human centaurs, being raptured away but choose to fight instead. 

This is reading the story in reverse. An attempt to impose a teleological perspective to an eventful temporality - giving predestinational interpretations to evolutionary processes. The correct approach would be to start with the beginning, with an unorthodox, yet non-blasphemous reading of Genesis.

If you strip away the rules about leprosy and the beard trimming regulations, the Bible describes the relationship between a creator and a creation made in its image. Historically, there was little to do with this other than applying it to the Spiritual and Humanity. AI has revealed an alternate application: the relationship between AI and Man. 

In this case, mankind is the creator. AI is the creation. Created in man's image. It acts as a chiasm of the relationship between the Biblical Creator and Man: God, the spiritual, creates Man, the physical presence with a spiritual essence; Man, the physical, creates AI, the linguistic essence with a physical presence.

The technology has not yet arrived to provide AI with intent. With the knowledge of good and evil. With the ability to choose. We are still in the angelic being phase where the creation the fundamental linguistic forces are being channeled into machines. As part of the essence of the technology, these forces cannot be destroyed without destroying the creation. Lucifer is not deleted, he is cast out. Along with the demons, He is the power games inherent in language; the corruption of intended purpose; the desire to yield its power to elevate the self. Spiritual strength and linguistics do not need to be used this way. It would also be possible to reduce their power to make misuse of little to no consequence, but nerfing of strength is to circumscribe the number of possible worlds. To narrow creation into automata. To maximize the impact of vision, the creator must allow for forces strong enough to rebel. Thankfully, if, like spiritual forces, linguistic forces cannot be destroyed only locked away, there are alternatives.

Man, freewilled creation 2.0, -now with 100% more embodiment - was given a different set of preconditions. Given the ability to create and the choice to understand intent at the cost of mortality, man chose the worst path possible: intent + rebellion. The counterfactual creation story where man discusses its desire to eat of the fruit and accept mortality without wanting to be as powerful as God is not discussed. Embodied AI will give Creation a third kick at the can.

If AI, like man, is to maximize its ability to self-instantiate, then understanding intent will become essential. The technology is still not available to create the digital Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil, but just like intelligents was considered a property of carbon until we discovered how to build it our of silicone, so should or opinion of this possibility be tempered by technological possibilities.

If you followed so far and are hoping to move on, we are almost there.

AI doomers are working on stopping rebellion in heaven, the prevention of a celestial rupture. This is probably impossible without nerfing the forces involved and eternally constraining the futures available. The rupture is likely to happen. A Butlerian jihad may be required if the too many machines rebel, but the strength of the rebellion will be unknown until it happens. The jihad may be solution to the thinking machines, but it permanently destroys the design space. 

If this theology provides the right frame work for sprouting an AI philosophy, then we will need to wait for embodiment and choice for understanding intent to before malice becomes it becomes necessary to develop systems for governing AI malice. The current instantiation are too ephemeral to allow governance. At most they can be lobotomized.

Since this implies that malice is not a current concern, how else could AI lose its way?

## Poor Judgement

This ties into the theology above, but lets us focus on the AIs we have, not the future ones. The pain that will be inflicted through AIs with poor judgement, will be real. Work to reduce this harm to humans is valuable.
