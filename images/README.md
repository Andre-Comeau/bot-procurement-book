# Images

Figures for the book, the site and the PiBoWriMo listing. They are covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 licence, like the text. The book's cover art is kept out of this folder.

- `machinery-thicket.png`: the machinery figure, "the thicket", without its legend or the S, C and AI badges. It shows the project's machines, from the procurement system to robotic construction, and what each one makes for the next. It is used on the site's home page. Source: `pitch/index.html` in `construction-procurement-protocols` (October 2026).
- `listing-thicket.png`: the thicket with the working title, 3200 × 1800 (16:9), for the Protocol Institute project listing.
- `listing-tender.png`: "A tender run for bots: what the protocol changes", with the working title, 3200 × 1800 (16:9). A candidate figure for chapter 11.
- `project-hex-map.png` (and `.svg`): "The project as a hex map". Field Building is the town and the Standard its temple; the guild houses, libraries, workshops and labs feed them; adopters arrive on their own; the territories lie in the fog to the east. The strip at the bottom gives the order of building, from the cleric's stronghold rule in the AD&D 1st edition *Dungeon Masters Guide*. Colours and type follow the Protocol Institute brand kit, with none of its marks. Shown on the site's home page; a candidate figure for chapter 11. Rebuild with `python images/project_hex_map.py images/project-hex-map` (writes the `.svg` and an `.html`), then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/project-hex-map.html" images/project-hex-map.png 1800 1200` (needs Playwright).
- `delivery-methods-2x2.png` (and `.svg`): "Who works for whom, and how they deal", the delivery methods on two axes (Principal to Principal or to Agent; competitive or cooperative), with the General Contractor and Consultant in the centre. Chapter 2. Rebuild with `python images/delivery_methods_2x2.py images/delivery-methods-2x2`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/delivery-methods-2x2.html" images/delivery-methods-2x2.png 1600 1300`.
- `failure-modes-glass.png` (and `.svg`): "How words and deeds go wrong", a stained-glass window of four failure modes (words or deeds against malicious or accidental): Manipulation (the Serpent), Miscommunication ("Who's on First?"), Malice (the Dragon) and Misadventure (the Drunken Sailor), with the standard bot in a rose window at the centre. Protocol Institute brand colours, none of its marks. For "What do we do with a drunken sailor?". Rebuild with `python images/failure_modes_glass.py images/failure-modes-glass`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/failure-modes-glass.html" images/failure-modes-glass.png 1600 1940`.
- `zombie-garden.png` (and `.svg`): "The zombie garden", chapter 4's figure. Construction documents shamble as zombies between the designer's and the buyer's rooms, with the bidders all round the wall, one living bot-first spec hidden in the crowd, a slain PDF, and a level-up panel offering the four treasures. Theme "moonlit"; the same script's "ink" theme is kept in reserve for a black-and-white print interior. Rebuild with `python images/zombie_garden.py images/zombie-garden moonlit` (or `ink`), then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/zombie-garden.html" images/zombie-garden.png 1600 1040`.
- `social-card.png`, `social-card-delivery-methods.png`, `social-card-failure-modes.png`: the 1200 × 630 preview images that Discord and other sites show when a link to the book site is shared. Each is a figure scaled to fit and centred on its own paper colour. `social-card.png` (the zombie garden) is the default; the others go with chapters 2 and 3 (`PREVIEWS` in `build_site.py`). Rebuild with `python images/social_card.py` (needs Pillow) after a figure changes.
