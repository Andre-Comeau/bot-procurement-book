# Images

Figures for the book, the site and the PiBoWriMo listing. They are covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 licence, like the text. The book's cover art is kept out of this folder.

- `machinery-thicket.png`: the machinery figure, "the thicket", without its legend or the S, C and AI badges. It shows the project's machines, from the procurement system to robotic construction, and what each one makes for the next. It is used on the site's home page. Source: `pitch/index.html` in `construction-procurement-protocols` (October 2026).
- `listing-thicket.png`: the thicket with the working title, 3200 × 1800 (16:9), for the Protocol Institute project listing.
- `listing-tender.png`: "A tender run for bots: what the protocol changes", with the working title, 3200 × 1800 (16:9). A candidate figure for chapter 11.
- `delivery-methods-2x2.png` (and `.svg`): "Who works for whom, and how they deal", the delivery methods on two axes (Principal to Principal or to Agent; competitive or cooperative), with the General Contractor and Consultant in the centre. Chapter 2. Rebuild with `python images/delivery_methods_2x2.py images/delivery-methods-2x2`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/delivery-methods-2x2.html" images/delivery-methods-2x2.png 1600 1300`.
- `failure-modes-glass.png` (and `.svg`): "How words and deeds go wrong", a stained-glass window of four failure modes (words or deeds against malicious or accidental): Manipulation (the Serpent), Miscommunication ("Who's on First?"), Malice (the Dragon) and Misadventure (the Drunken Sailor), with the standard bot in a rose window at the centre. Protocol Institute brand colours, none of its marks. For "What do we do with a drunken sailor?". Rebuild with `python images/failure_modes_glass.py images/failure-modes-glass`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/failure-modes-glass.html" images/failure-modes-glass.png 1600 1940`.
- `zombie-garden.png` (and `.svg`): "The zombie garden", chapter 4's figure. Construction documents shamble as zombies between the designer's and the buyer's rooms, with the bidders all round the wall, one living bot-first spec hidden in the crowd, a slain PDF, and a level-up panel offering the four treasures. Theme "moonlit"; the same script's "ink" theme is kept in reserve for a black-and-white print interior. Rebuild with `python images/zombie_garden.py images/zombie-garden moonlit` (or `ink`), then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/zombie-garden.html" images/zombie-garden.png 1600 1040`.
- `social-card.png`, `social-card-delivery-methods.png`, `social-card-failure-modes.png`, `social-card-second-opinion.png`, `social-card-world-2.png` (chapter 11), `social-card-one-losing-job.png` (the underwriters' chapter and Appendix E), `social-card-oak-falls.png` (chapter 6), `social-card-three-parties.png` (chapter 6a): the 1200 × 630 preview images that Discord and other sites show when a link to the book site is shared. Each is a figure scaled to fit and centred on its own paper colour. `social-card.png` (the zombie garden) is the default; the others go with chapters 2 and 3 (`PREVIEWS` in `build_site.py`). Rebuild with `python images/social_card.py` (needs Pillow) after a figure changes.
- `world-2-standards.png` (and `.svg`): "The Standard", World 2 of the overworld map, for chapter 11. Each level is an existing standard with the change the book would ask of it (field building; OCDS and OC4IDS; IFC 4.3 and IDS; methods of measurement; CCDC 23; ISO 10845-3; AACE's estimate classes; IEEE 3119), leading to a castle (a new part of ISO 10845 for the reasonable bot) and a courthouse (the first case). Same style as chapter 1's `second-opinion.png`, whose drawing helpers it reuses. Rebuild with `python images/world_2_standards.py images/world-2-standards`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/world-2-standards.html" images/world-2-standards.png 1800 1160`.
- `testing-process.png` (and `.svg`): "How the estimator is checked", chapter 1's figure for "What the scores say", as three stained-glass windows side by side, from older contracts to newer: 1 Learn (blue, a book; Texas contracts let before May 2026), 2 Rehearse (green, a circle of arrows; from May 2026, scored by three versions) and 3 Final test (pale rust, a padlock, tagged "not run yet"; newer bid results piling up online). Each panel names the researchers' term (training, validation, test data). The same glass as `failure-modes-glass.png`, in the Protocol Institute brand colours with none of its marks. Rebuild with `python images/testing_process.py images/testing-process`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/testing-process.html" images/testing-process.png 1700 1250`. Earlier versions (flat panels, then an overworld map) are in the git history (commits f06cc15 and 34d45eb).
- `one-losing-job.png` (and `.svg`): "One losing job is not a claim", the underwriters' chapter figure for "The insurable event". Completed jobs are mugs filled to the margin each made; a normal year has a few short mugs but an average above the trigger (no payment), a bad year an average below it (the insurer pays its share, the bidder carries the rest). Schematic, not data; the book palette as solid colours. Rebuild with `python images/one_losing_job.py images/one-losing-job` (writes the `.svg` and an `.html`), then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/one-losing-job.html" images/one-losing-job.png 1600 1300`.
- `tender-api-door.png` (and `.svg`): "One door between two rooms", chapter 6's figure for the redescription of the tender as an interface. The design room (the engineer's beam) and the construction room (the builder's crew schedule, with a surety standing behind the builder), and between them the tender as a door made of a stack of PDFs, the call (the bill of quantities) going one way and the answer (the bid) coming back. Book palette as solid colours. Rebuild with `python images/tender_api_door.py images/tender-api-door`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/tender-api-door.html" images/tender-api-door.png 1600 1100`.
- `oak-falls.png` (and `.svg`): "The oak falls", chapter 6's figure for "The cost of a call" and "The oak falls". Left, the cost of bidding as an oak, with a few large bidders and the plan room grown in its shade and the rest shaded out; right, the stump, the fallen trunk, light pouring into the clearing and a thicket racing for it, over a forest floor that is the market's record. Also the chapter's link card (`social-card-oak-falls.png`). Rebuild with `python images/oak_falls.py images/oak-falls`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/oak-falls.html" images/oak-falls.png 1600 1000`.
- `four-doors.png` (and `.svg`): "Four ways to answer a flooded door", chapter 6's figure for the section of that name. Cap, key, fee and the published check as four doors, each with its device (a plate reading two a month and a rope barrier; a keyhole and a key on a hook; a slot marked insert bond and a coin; a door standing open with the rules posted beside it), its online twin, its construction twin and the chapter's verdict. Rebuild with `python images/four_doors.py images/four-doors`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/four-doors.html" images/four-doors.png 1600 1210`.
- `slack-other-option.png` (and `.svg`): "Slack, and the other option", chapter 6's figure for the section of that name. A schematic chart, not data: bids per tender against the eyes available to read them, from the plan room through the plan room online to AI making the call nearly free; the attention line stays flat while the bids climb past it, the gap below marked slack and the gap above the thicket; beside it Galbraith's two choices as two lists of where the book finds each, add slack (the cushion, the long tender, the oak, the contingency, the supplier of one, the margin, Taylorism's plateau) and raise the capacity (the second opinion, the tender as data, the same check, the record, the open door, the evaluator and keyholder, insurance on the margin, the standard). Rebuild with `python images/slack_other_option.py images/slack-other-option`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/slack-other-option.html" images/slack-other-option.png 1600 1240`.
- `three-parties.png` (and `.svg`): "Three parties at the door", the shared figure of chapters 6a to 6c, placed at the top of 6a. Three panels, one per chapter, each with an icon (the buyers' door and a stack of bids taller than the reader; the bidders' thicket leaning to a sun; the vendors' counter with a whistle, a shovel with a price tag and a sun marked not for sale), the party's question, and three lists in the chapters' own words shortened: what they need, what a published check gives them, the catch. Under the three, a band for the answer they cannot give each other, since they meet as principals: the standard and the insurer. Also 6a's link card (`social-card-three-parties.png`). Rebuild with `python images/three_parties.py images/three-parties`, then `node images/project_hex_map_render.js "$PWD/images/three-parties.html" images/three-parties.png 1600 1530`.
