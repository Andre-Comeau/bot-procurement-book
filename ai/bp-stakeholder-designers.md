# Stakeholders: architects, engineers and testers

> Outline. The argument below is a draft awaiting the author's review. Bot Procurement is a proposal.

## In short

- **The offer:** less administration under fixed fees. Contract administration is a large share of a design fee, and requests for information (RFIs) on a building project run into the hundreds, each taking hours. Saved design hours billed by the hour are no draw; saved administration hours under a fixed fee are.
- **The test of documents:** several independent bots each build their own model of the project from the documents, alone. Where they agree, the documents say one thing; where they differ, the differences locate the ambiguity. A pass means clear, not right.
- **Three groups:** architects (fewer coordination questions), engineers (quantities counted twice before tender), and testers (independent checkers, for whom this is revenue, and who would be the third parties qualifying reference applications).
- **Today's limit:** benchmarks of AI editing building models in 2026 show the best systems far from reliable.
