# Stakeholders: the trades, and who holds the jig

> A jig holds the work and the tool in the right relation, so a hard cut can be made again, safely, by anyone on the crew. The person who knows the cut builds it.

## In short

- **"The trades"** means trade contractors (mechanical, electrical, concrete, drywall) who price one part and bid to the general contractor; tradespeople who do the work, many in unions; and the trades' institutions (associations, depositories, unions, training bodies).
- **One level down:** the general contractor builds its bid from trade prices, so most of a building tender was priced by people who never see the owner. A bot's misreading runs downhill: the duct nobody priced because each trade thought it was the other's is the oldest argument on site. Trades are near the back of the line when a contractor fails.
- **The trades' own protocols:** in Canadian places that run a bid depository, trades send sealed, time-stamped prices to the depository, not to the general contractors; in Yukon the depository clock is the official time and copies are kept at least a year. Everyone hurries to be last so a price cannot leak to a rival.
- **More people manage than build:** in Australia, construction managers rose from about 61,000 to about 131,000 between 2006 and 2025 while trades workers rose from about 345,000 to about 414,000; in the US the trades' share of the construction workforce fell from 71 to under 61 percent between 2005 and 2022 while managers rose from 10 to 16 percent. Why is not known.
- **The project's aim:** the office layer of administration and documentation, not the trades' work.

## The jigbot

The book's word for a robot the trades control: "a jigbot is what a tradesperson builds when they set up and teach a robot for the task at hand, the way they'd build a jig." It is a common noun, not a third name for the field ([the two names](bp-two-names.md)). The open questions it raises are the trades': who has authority to program the machine, how apprenticeship changes, and making sure the bill for harm does not fall automatically on the tradesperson.

## A limit stated plainly

The project's price method reads public prime bids. Trade prices are private and the project has no data on them; what it says about trade prices comes from people, not the method.
