# Stakeholders: what construction buyers stand to gain

> Buyers meet the flood of AI-written bids first. Bot Procurement is a proposal; its test has not been run.

## In short

- **The pitch:** a published check that gives the same answer for the same bid, so more bids do not need more eyes; a second opinion on every change price; and a record a legal department can defend.
- **The catch:** the buyer is the natural first insurer of the reference price ([buyer as insurer](bp-buyer-as-insurer.md)), and a public buyer must keep the door open to all qualified bidders.

## Five buyers, five doors

| Buyer | Their door | Exposure to the flood |
|---|---|---|
| **Highway agency** | Measures the work itself; bids are prices per item and travel as data files; prequalifies and bonds bidders | Lowest for priced bids; the written parts (design-build proposals, qualifications) are exposed |
| **Public building owner** (school, hospital, water plant) | Drawings and specifications; each bidder measures its own quantities; bids come back as PDFs | Highest: evaluation is all reading |
| **Megaproject procurer** (P3, large design-build) | Months or years to procure; in Ontario, firms answering calls fell from more than five to about two and a half in a decade, and four billion-dollar projects drew a single bid each | The flood looks like polish: proposals matched to the criteria |
| **Private owner** | Can shut the door and pay a premium for established firms | Low; wants speed and confidence, not protest-proof decisions |
| **Buyer's agent** (construction manager, owner's consultant) | Answers to the owner for every award | Their discretion is the first thing a losing bidder questions |

## What they need

- **Volume without hiring.** Opening tenders to the widest field guards against nepotism and bid-fixing, but staffing up human review is politically sensitive.
- **Decisions that survive a challenge.** In US protest law, a protester who shows two substantively indistinguishable proposals were scored differently can win; AI makes proposals alike, so small scoring differences matter more. AI may be more essential as a back-checker of claims than as a writer of them.
- **The door kept open.** Trade law lets a public buyer ask for capacity that fits the job, not for a firm to be established; Europe caps the turnover a buyer may demand at twice the contract value, and a pending proposal would lower that and allow drawing lots among qualified firms.
- **Faster change reviews** ([the change-pricing problem](bp-change-pricing-problem.md)).

## Aside: a one-page ask to buyers (draft, not yet agreed)

Run the [evaluator](bp-evaluator.md) beside your current fair-and-reasonable review on finished changes; compare its flags with what your reviewers found; and keep the record. Nothing changes in your process until the comparison says it should.
